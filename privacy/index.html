MezmurFaith Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

MezmurFaith lets you read mezmur lyrics, keep favorites, and, if you wish, send in a mezmur or report a problem. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

Your favorites, collections and settings stay on your device. Information leaves your device only when you choose to send a mezmur or a problem report, and when the app asks our server for the latest published mezmur.

Who we are

MezmurFaith is published by an individual developer, not by a company. In this policy, “we” and “our” mean that developer. You can reach us using the details in the Contact section.

What stays on your device

The app saves these on your device only. None of it is sent to us:

  • Your favorites and recently viewed mezmur.
  • The collections you create (their names and the mezmur in them).
  • Your settings: language, text size, appearance, and whether reminders are on.
  • A saved copy of the latest published mezmur list, so it still shows when you have no connection.

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

Reminders

If you turn on observance reminders, iOS asks your permission to show notifications. The reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS, and nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

If you submit a mezmur

Submitting is optional. When you submit, the app sends:

  • The title and the lyrics.
  • The category you chose, or the name of a new category you suggest.
  • The language and the singer or performer, if you fill them in.
  • A random ID the app creates for that submission, so a retry is not saved twice.
  • The app version and the iOS version.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, account, device identifier or location. Please do not put personal information in the title, lyrics, singer or category fields: reviewers read them, and approved mezmur are shown publicly.

A new submission is private and waits for review. Only authorized reviewers can read it. If a reviewer approves it, it is published in the app: its title, lyrics, language, singer and category are shown to everyone who uses the app, the next time their app refreshes. A reviewer can later remove an approved mezmur from the app. Submissions that are not approved are never shown to other people.

If you report a problem

Reporting is optional. When you report a problem with a mezmur, the app sends:

  • The mezmur's title and its internal ID.
  • The type of problem you chose.
  • Your description (up to 500 characters).
  • A random ID the app creates for that report, so a retry is not saved twice.
  • The app version and the iOS version.

No name, email address or account is needed, and reports are not shown to other people. They are stored so we can review and fix the problem.

Network information and spam protection

Whenever the app contacts our server (to check for new mezmur, to submit, or to report), the server receives your internet connection's network address, as any internet service does. Our submission and report services do not save that address.

To limit spam, the submission service counts how many submissions come from the same connection each day. It turns the network address into a scrambled code, using a secret value kept on the server, and saves only that code, the date and a count. Counts older than two days are removed by a cleanup that runs occasionally, so an entry can be kept a little longer than that. The report service also limits repeated sends, but only in the server's memory for a short time (up to an hour); nothing is written to the database for that.

Our hosting provider, Supabase, may keep its own standard technical logs of requests (such as the time and the network address) under its own policies.

Reviewers

A small number of authorized reviewers sign in with an email address, a password and a one-time code, through our authentication provider, Supabase. This applies only to reviewers, not to people who use the app.

When a reviewer makes a decision, we record which reviewer made it, when, and the change of status. A reviewer can also add a private note to a submission. The record of decisions is kept even if a submission is later permanently deleted, and it contains no lyrics.

Who handles the information

We use Supabase to host the database and the server functions that receive submissions and reports and provide the published mezmur list.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not share information with advertisers. If you use Share, iOS shows its own share sheet and you choose where the text goes; we do not receive it.

How long information is kept

  • Information that stays on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Submissions that are not approved are kept until an authorized reviewer deletes them. A rejected submission can be permanently deleted.
  • Approved mezmur stay published until a reviewer removes them.
  • Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them.
  • Spam-protection counts are described above.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without sending anything.
  • You can delete the information on your device by deleting the app, and turn reminders off at any time.

To ask us to remove something you sent, contact us with its title and roughly when you sent it. Because the app has no accounts, we can find a submission or report only from the details you give us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy in the app and on its web page, and change the “Last updated” date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions, or a request to remove something you sent, can go to mezmurfaith@gmail.com.
