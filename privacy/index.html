MezmurFaith Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the MezmurFaith app (the “App”) handles information. The App does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Your favorites, collections and settings are stored on your device. Information is transmitted only when you choose to submit a mezmur or report a problem, and when the App retrieves the latest published mezmur from our server.

About MezmurFaith

MezmurFaith is developed and published by an individual developer, not by a company. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that developer. Contact details are provided in the Contact section below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Favorites and recently viewed mezmur.
  • Collections you create, including their names and contents.
  • Preferences, including language, text size, appearance and reminder settings.
  • A saved copy of the published mezmur list, so that it remains available without a connection.

This information is removed when you delete the App.

Notifications

If you turn on observance reminders, iOS will ask for your permission to display notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS, and no information about them is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the App’s settings or in iOS Settings.

Mezmur Submissions

Submitting a mezmur is optional. A submission includes:

  • The title and lyrics.
  • The selected category, or the name of a suggested new category.
  • The language and the singer or performer, if provided.
  • A random ID created by the App for that submission, so that a resent submission is not stored twice.
  • The App version and iOS version.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, account, device identifier or location. Please do not include personal information in the title, lyrics, singer or category fields: submissions are read by reviewers, and approved mezmur are displayed publicly.

New submissions are private and remain pending until they are reviewed. Only authorized reviewers can access them. If a submission is approved, its title, lyrics, language, singer and category are published in the App and become visible to all users the next time their App refreshes. A reviewer may later remove an approved mezmur from the App. Submissions that are not approved are never shown to other users.

Problem Reports

Reporting a problem with a mezmur is optional. A report includes:

  • The mezmur’s title and its internal ID.
  • The type of problem you selected.
  • Your description, up to 500 characters.
  • A random ID created by the App for that report, so that a resent report is not stored twice.
  • The App version and iOS version.

Reports do not require a name, email address or account, and they are not shown to other users. They are retained so that the reported problem can be reviewed and corrected.

Network Information and Spam Prevention

Whenever the App communicates with our server, to check for newly published mezmur or to send a submission or report, the server receives your network address, as is standard for any internet service. Our submission and report services do not store that address.

To prevent spam, the submission service limits the number of submissions from a single connection each day. It converts the network address into a one-way code, using a secret value held on the server, and stores only that code, the date and a count. Counts older than two days are removed by a periodic cleanup, so an individual entry may be kept slightly longer. The report service also limits repeated reports, but only temporarily in the server’s memory, for up to an hour; nothing is written to the database for this purpose.

Our hosting provider, Supabase, may retain standard technical logs of requests, such as the time and the network address, under its own policies.

Reviewer Accounts

A limited number of authorized reviewers sign in with an email address, a password and a one-time code, through our authentication provider, Supabase. These accounts are used only by reviewers; people who use the App do not have accounts.

When a reviewer acts on a submission, we record which reviewer acted, when, and the resulting change of status. A reviewer may also add a private note to a submission. This record of decisions is kept even if a submission is later permanently deleted, and it contains no lyrics.

Service Providers

We use Supabase to host the database and the server functions that receive submissions and reports and provide the published mezmur list.

The App does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and we do not share information with advertisers. When you use Share, iOS presents its own share sheet and you choose where the content is sent; we do not receive it.

Data Retention

  • Information stored on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the App.
  • Submissions that are not approved are kept until an authorized reviewer deletes them. A rejected submission can be permanently deleted.
  • Approved mezmur remain published until a reviewer removes them.
  • Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them.
  • Spam-prevention counts are kept as described above.

Your Choices

  • You can use the App without sending any information.
  • You can delete the information stored on your device by deleting the App, and you can turn off reminders at any time.

To request removal of something you submitted, contact us with its title and the approximate date you sent it. Because the App has no user accounts, we can identify a submission or report only from the details you provide.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this policy in the App and on its web page, and revise the “Last updated” date shown at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy, or to request removal of information you submitted, please contact mezmurfaith@gmail.com.
