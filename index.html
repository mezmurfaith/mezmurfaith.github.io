MezmurFaith

Mezmur lyrics in Ge’ez and Latin script, with the Eritrean and Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo fasting and feast calendar.

Support

For questions, corrections to a mezmur, or requests to remove information you submitted, please contact MezmurFaith Support at mezmurfaith@gmail.com.

To report a problem with a specific mezmur, open its lyrics in the App and use the report button.
