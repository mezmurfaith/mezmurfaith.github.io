MezmurFaith

Mezmur lyrics in Tigrinya and Latin script, with the Orthodox fasting and feast calendar.

Support

For questions, corrections to a mezmur, or a request to remove something you sent, email mezmurfaith@gmail.com.

You can also report a problem with a mezmur from inside the app: open its lyrics and tap the report button.
